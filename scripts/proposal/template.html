The new site & running costs

How the new site works

Next.js

The website framework used by many of the web's largest sites. Pages are built ahead of time, so they load fast on any phone. Photos are resized and compressed automatically, and nothing jumps while the page loads.

Cloudflare

Hosts the site free on its Workers plan, from 300+ locations worldwide. Static pages are served with no request limit, and dynamic requests get 100,000 a day. Free SSL included.
